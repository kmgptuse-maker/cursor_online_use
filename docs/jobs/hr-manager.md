## About the role

We are hiring an HR Manager to own the day-to-day people operation and to partner with leaders on hiring, performance, employee relations, and culture. This is a hands-on manager role: you will set process, coach managers, handle sensitive issues with judgment, and keep the company compliant as we grow.

You will be the primary HR point of contact for [function / region / company]. Success looks like managers who know how to hire and develop people, employees who trust the process, and HR systems that scale without becoming bureaucracy.

---

## What you will do

### People operations and employee lifecycle

- Own end-to-end employee lifecycle: onboarding, transfers, promotions, leaves, and offboarding.
- Keep employee records, contracts, and HRIS data accurate, complete, and confidential.
- Run or improve core people programs (onboarding, performance cycles, compensation reviews, engagement surveys).
- Partner with Finance/Payroll on compensation changes, benefits administration, and headcount reporting.

### Talent and recruiting support

- Partner with hiring managers on workforce planning, job descriptions, interview loops, and offer decisions.
- Ensure recruiting is fair, consistent, and compliant (job postings, interviews, background checks, offers).
- Help close candidates with a clear, professional candidate experience.
- Track hiring metrics (time-to-fill, source quality, offer accept rate) and flag bottlenecks.

### Manager coaching and performance

- Coach managers on feedback, performance conversations, promotions, and PIP design.
- Facilitate performance review cycles: calibration, documentation, and follow-through.
- Escalate and resolve performance issues before they become legal or culture problems.
- Train new managers on people basics (1:1s, goal setting, documentation, leave, accommodations).

### Employee relations and culture

- Be the first point of contact for employee relations: conflict, conduct, harassment, and policy questions.
- Investigate issues promptly, fairly, and with clear documentation.
- Advise leaders on org design, team health, and change (reorgs, role changes, RIFs if needed).
- Support engagement, inclusion, and recognition programs that actually get used.

### Compliance, policy, and risk

- Own or maintain the employee handbook, policies, and standard templates (offers, PIPs, warnings).
- Ensure employment practices comply with applicable labor law in [country / states / countries of operation].
- Coordinate leaves, accommodations, and workplace safety with legal and operations as needed.
- Reduce people risk: consistent process, audit-ready files, and timely escalation of high-risk cases.

### Team leadership (if applicable)

- Manage, coach, and develop [HR generalists / recruiters / people ops coordinators].
- Set priorities, SLAs, and quality standards for the HR function.
- Choose and improve HR tools (HRIS, ATS, payroll, engagement) without tool sprawl.

## What you bring

### Required

- 5+ years in HR, people operations, or a related people role, including 2+ years owning employee relations or generalist HR for a team or site.
- Experience managing full-cycle people processes (hiring, onboarding, performance, offboarding), not only recruiting or only admin.
- Working knowledge of employment law and HR compliance in [primary jurisdiction]; you know when to handle vs. when to involve counsel.
- Proven judgment on sensitive issues: you document well, stay calm, and do not gossip.
- Strong written communication: clear policies, investigation notes, and manager guidance.
- Comfort with HRIS/ATS tools and basic people analytics (headcount, turnover, hiring funnel).
- Ability to partner with executives and frontline managers without becoming a bottleneck.

### Preferred

- Direct people-management experience (you have managed at least one HR/People report).
- Experience in [industry: tech / manufacturing / healthcare / professional services / etc.].
- Multi-state or multi-country employment experience.
- SHRM-CP, SHRM-SCP, PHR, SPHR, or equivalent.
- Experience standing up or professionalizing HR at a growth-stage company (handbook, performance cycle, compensation bands).
- Familiarity with compensation frameworks, job leveling, and pay equity reviews.

### How you work

- Practical: you prefer a simple process that gets used over a perfect process that sits in a deck.
- Discreet: employees and leaders can bring you hard problems and trust the outcome.
- Direct: you tell managers the truth early, with options, not after the fact.
- Organized: tickets, cases, and cycles do not slip.
- Fair: you apply policy consistently and can explain exceptions.

## Success in the first 6–12 months

**First 90 days**

- Know the org: roles, managers, open issues, and current people processes.
- Close obvious gaps (missing contracts, inconsistent offers, stale handbook, messy HRIS).
- Establish a clear intake path for manager and employee questions.

**By 6 months**

- Managers use a consistent hiring, onboarding, and performance playbook.
- Employee relations cases are tracked, timely, and documented.
- Core metrics exist and are reviewed with leadership (turnover, time-to-fill, eNPS/engagement, case volume).

**By 12 months**

- HR is trusted: leaders come early, employees know how to get help, and audits would not scare us.
- People programs run on a calendar, not as one-off fire drills.
- The function can scale to the next stage of headcount without a full rebuild.
